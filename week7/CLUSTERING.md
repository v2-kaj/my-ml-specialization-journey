What is clustering?

Grouping items that are similar into n groups.
A clustering algorithm looks at data points and automatically groups similar data points into groups.

In supervised learning, logistic regression groups data points as well given inputs data x and target labels y as its training data.

In unsupervised learning, clustering algorithms are only given input data x but not target labels y as its training data.

Applications of clustering.
1. Grouping similar news articles
2. Market segmentation
3. DNA Analysis - analysing dna expression 
etc
